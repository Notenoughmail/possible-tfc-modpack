# Quartz Tools

<ItemImage id="certus_quartz_cutting_knife" scale="4" />

Cutting knives are used to make <ItemLink id="name_press" />es, <ItemLink id="cable_anchor" />s, and regular [Inscriber Press](../items-blocks-machines/presses.md)es

To craft a name press, right click the cutting knife and insert an ingot of steel or wrought iron, you will then have to type the name you
wish to write onto the plate then simply extract the finished plate.
